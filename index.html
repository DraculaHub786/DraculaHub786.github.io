Security first,

Most of my work starts with a threat model and ends with a tool someone can actually run. That habit came from building honeypots, SIEM pipelines and file-encryption services — the kind of software where a quiet bug is worse than a loud crash.

Before that I spent time on applied ML and NLP, which is where SkillGap AI and the AQI health planner came from. These days I care most about the seam between the two: AI that ships, and security tooling that doesn't get in the way.
